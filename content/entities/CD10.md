# CD10

## Overview

CD10 (neutral endopeptidase; neprilysin) is expressed on B cell precursors, transitional B cells, and germinal centre B cells in humans. Its expression marks immature or GC-associated B cells; absence of CD10 in combination with IgD⁻CD27⁻ phenotype is used to confirm that DN B cells are not transitional or pre-GC contaminants.

## Key Points from Literature

- DN B cells are CD10⁻ in peripheral blood, distinguishing them from transitional B cells (CD10⁺ CD38^high IgD⁺), pre-GC cells (CD10⁺ CD38^high IgD⁺/⁻), and GC cells in tonsil (CD10⁺ CD38^high) (see [[Wei2007 - DN Memory B Cells in SLE]], 8-color multiparameter analysis).
- In tonsil, the DN counterpart (IgD⁻CD27⁻CD10⁻ Bm5 fraction) is distinguished from tonsillar GC cells (IgD⁻CD27⁻CD10⁺ CD38^high) by CD10 and CD38 expression (see [[Wei2007 - DN Memory B Cells in SLE]]).
- A small fraction of isotype-switched CD27⁺ and DN cells in PBL express CD10, likely representing recirculating pre-GC cells (see [[Wei2007 - DN Memory B Cells in SLE]]).
- **CD10⁻ in acN cells:** The absence of CD10 expression on activated naive (acN) cells (IgD⁺CD27⁻MTG⁺CD24⁻CD21⁻) was used to argue against misclassification of these cells as late transitional B cells, which can share IgD⁺MTG⁺ phenotype with acN cells but are CD10⁺. CD10⁻ status thus helps confirm the acN cell identity alongside CD24⁻ and CD38^lo (see [[Tipton2015 - ASC Diversity and Origin in SLE]]).

- **CD10⁻ gate for mature B cells in dengue:** GarciaBates2013 gated on CD19⁺CD10⁻ cells to define the mature B cell population before further subset analysis by CD27/CD21/CD20/CD38. This excludes transitional and GC-derived B cells from the analysis and is a standard approach for dengue B cell phenotyping (see [[GarciaBates2013 - Plasmablast Response and Dengue Severity]], LSRII FCM, n=84 dengue).

- **CD10 is named as one of the exclusion markers that keeps transitional cells out of a CD21ˡᵒ activation gate.** The review warns that early transitional (T1) B cells are themselves CD21ˡᵒ and must be excluded using [[CD38]], [[CD24]] **and/or CD10** — without that exclusion, a CD21-anchored DN2 or activated-memory gate can be contaminated from below by developmentally immature cells. Table 1 gives T1/T2 as CD10⁺⁺/⁺ (see [[Sanz2019 - Consistent Classification of Human B Cell Populations]], review — **no original data**). See [[DN2 Gating Strategy]].
- **CD10 used as an exclusion gate to restrict analysis to mature B cells** — all populations defined as CD10⁻ (CD10 APC, clone CB-CALLA) before CD21/CD27 subsetting, excluding transitional and immature cells from the atypical-MBC frequency denominator. ⚠ Note the microarray arm's magnetic fractionation had **no CD10 step**, so those fractions were not depleted of transitional cells (see [[Portugal2015 - Malaria Atypical MBCs Lack BCR Signaling and Effector Function]], n=107 flow / n=20 microarray).
- **CD10 as the subtraction term, and an unexplained finding it produced.** In the founding malaria atypical-MBC panel, CD10 does no positive work — it is used to **subtract** immature cells from the CD21⁻CD27⁻ and CD21⁺CD27⁻ gates, and separately as a **tetrameric depletion reagent** in the magnetic fractionation used for the functional assays (see [[Weiss2009 - Atypical Memory B Cells Expanded in Malaria-Endemic Area]], n=75 Malian + n=10 US). In passing, the panel reports **immature CD10⁺ B cells lower in Malian adults** than in either US adults or Malian children (p < 0.001), which the paper notes and does not pursue. Whether chronic infection alters marrow B cell output is untested anywhere in this wiki's corpus.
