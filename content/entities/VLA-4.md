# VLA-4

## Overview

VLA-4 (α4β1, CD49d/CD29, encoded by *Itga4* and *Itgb1*) is a β1 integrin whose ligand is VCAM-1. With [[LFA-1]] it retains T-bet⁺CD11c⁺ B cells at the splenic marginal zone. The two are blocked together in the only experiment that demonstrates the mechanism, so their individual contributions are not separated.

## Key Points from Literature

- T-bet⁺CD11c⁺ B cells showed **elevated transcript and surface protein expression of VLA-4** compared with naive follicular B cells (see [[Song2022 - Tfh Outside Germinal Centers Drive T-bet CD11c B Cells]], murine, day 15 post-LCMV)
- *Itga4* and *Itgb1* were upregulated in T-bet⁺CD11c⁺ cells relative to both GC and naive B cells (see [[Song2022 - Tfh Outside Germinal Centers Drive T-bet CD11c B Cells]], murine RNA-seq, day 12)
- **In vivo blockade of the LFA-1 and VLA-4 alpha subunits for 3 hours caused significant loss of these cells from spleen with a concomitant rise in blood** (see [[Song2022 - Tfh Outside Germinal Centers Drive T-bet CD11c B Cells]], murine, 3–4 mice/group)
- VLA-4 engages **VCAM-1** at the marginal zone (see [[Song2022 - Tfh Outside Germinal Centers Drive T-bet CD11c B Cells]], citing prior work)
- In a different compartment, **α4β1 and α6β1 interactions with VCAM-1** are implicated in bone marrow memory B cell residency (see [[Glaros2025 - Multilayered Identity of B Cell Memory]], review) — suggesting the same adhesion pair supports B cell residency at more than one site

## Contradictions & Debates

None recorded — single-source murine finding, no counter-evidence, no human replication. See the scope caution on [[LFA-1]].
